Frequently Asked Questions

A QR code (Quick Response code) is a two-dimensional barcode that can store various types of data, including URLs, text, contact information, and more. It can be scanned by smartphones and other devices with QR code readers.

Click the "Click or drag to upload logo" area to select an image file (PNG, JPG, or SVG). The logo will be automatically scaled and placed in the center of your QR code. We recommend using a high error correction level (H) when adding a logo to ensure scannability.

The error correction level determines how much of the QR code can be damaged or obscured and still be scannable. Use L (7%) for minimal errors, M (15%) for general use, Q (25%) for more robustness, or H (30%) for maximum error correction, especially when adding logos.
